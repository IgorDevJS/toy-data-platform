# Fase 5 · Sensibilidade: e se a janela inicial fosse 2015–2017 (mais títulos com nota)?

# Mesmo cálculo de 5-04 (Trino, IC 95%, Welch), só os 3 gêneros que mais cresceram e o catálogo.
# 2017 sozinho tem 1188 títulos e 192 com linha no fct_titles, mais que 2015–16 juntos (77).

## definição escolhida (confere com 5-04): inicial 2015–2016 × final 2019–2020
$ trino --execute "select b.genre_key as genre, case when c.added_year between 2015 and 2016 then 'inicial' else 'final' end as janela, count(t.rating) as n, round(avg(t.rating), 3) as media, round(stddev_samp(t.rating), 3) as dp from refined.ml.fct_catalog c join refined.ml.bridge_title_genre b on b.title_key = c.title_key left join refined.ml.fct_titles t on t.title_key = c.title_key where (c.added_year between 2015 and 2016 or c.added_year between 2019 and 2020) and b.genre_key in ('International Movies', 'Dramas', 'Comedies') group by 1, 2 union all select 'CATALOGO_INTEIRO', case when c.added_year between 2015 and 2016 then 'inicial' else 'final' end, count(t.rating) as n, round(avg(t.rating), 3) as media, round(stddev_samp(t.rating), 3) as dp from refined.ml.fct_catalog c left join refined.ml.fct_titles t on t.title_key = c.title_key where (c.added_year between 2015 and 2016 or c.added_year between 2019 and 2020) group by 1, 2 order by 1, 2 desc"  (2026-11-16 20:25:45)
